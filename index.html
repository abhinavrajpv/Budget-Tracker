Install on Android Phone

To install VaultBudget on your Android phone as a standalone app:

  1. Open your deployed GitHub Pages link in Google Chrome on your Android phone.
  2. Tap the three vertical dots (⋮) in Chrome's top-right corner.
  3. Tap "Install app" (or "Add to Home screen").
  4. Confirm by tapping Install.
✔ The app will be placed directly in your Android App Drawer.
✔ Works 100% offline with zero data consumption.
✔ No Google Play Store account required.
